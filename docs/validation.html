Power transition acceptance concept

The intended design switches priority from the battery to an attached standard USB supply and back again, without an MCU reset during normal operation. The active acceptance context records a battery-pack terminal condition of 1.80 V under load and a 3V3_SYS design load of 280 mA.

A meaningful integrated test must observe both input currents, regulated supply rails, MCU VDD and NRST, SD supply and real write completion. An artificial waveform or a model with inconsistent current accounting must not be called a hardware PASS.

Why the one-layer experiment is separate

The laser-process isolation coupon tests achievable copper separation first. The mux/load carrier is a potential next physical test. The buck and boost sections have more sensitive ground and switching-loop constraints, so manufacturability on a single copper layer remains unqualified.

This experimental track does not replace the two-layer RevA baseline or remove the need for proper power-stage verification.

How to interpret an old PASS label

Every PASS must be read with its scope and original evidence. A script unit test passing, a DRC-clean layout, and a prototype surviving a real source switchover are three different claims. Historical archived results may be superseded.

For the exact next step and latest limitations use the live CURRENT_HANDOFF.md; the T25DA feasibility report records the current single-layer investigation.
