## T25F source-selection interpretation (static fixture only)

Rechecking the exact 30 kΩ/10 kΩ `R_PR_TOP/R_PR_BOTTOM` in `01_POWER.SchDoc` and the [TI TPS2116 datasheet Rev. A](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2116.pdf) resolves the final state: VIN1=3.3 V gives PR1=0.825 V, **below** the 0.92–1.08 V PR1 reference. In priority mode the datasheet truth table selects VIN2; the observed VOUT=4.944 V near VIN2=5 V and ST=0.03267 V (status low for VIN2) are consistent with that static selection. Ideal divider + VREF and resistor tolerances give a **3.639–4.369 V** VIN1 static boundary, nominal 4.000 V (details in T25F matrix). This explains the observed fixture state; the fixed sources never crossed the boundary, so no dynamic switchover timing/dip was tested.
