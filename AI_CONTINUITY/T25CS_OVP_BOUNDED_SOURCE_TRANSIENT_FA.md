# T25CS — Bounded OVP pass-through transient: executable RC source-envelope diagnostic

**Date:** 2026-10-09. **Project:** Datalogger RevA. **Baseline:** GitHub main `ab1fffd7606df05981c3b2d5ac15e3453b327aac` (T25CR). **Result classification:** `ACTUAL_TEST_WITH_RAW_EVIDENCE / BOUNDED_BEHAVIORAL_SIMULATION_NO_HARDWARE_PASS`; real TS431/PNP/FSS2301S OVP and no-reset are `BLOCKED_BY_NAMED_MODEL_OR_INSTRUMENT`. **No CAD ECO / no procurement / fabrication HOLD.**

## Scope of the *new* run

This is a deliberately **different fixture** from invalid T25AE/T25BT/T25CO and from completed T25CP. It computes an exact, first-order *external hypothetical RC pass-through* response when an idealized source ramps from USB 5.5 V to a 12 V fault over 1, 10 or 100 microseconds. Before the assumed ideal instantaneous cutoff the protected output is connected through a fixed resistance to a capacitor; input source and protected output are NOT assumed equal and the full ramp response is integrated analytically. Model charge conservation: `dVprotected/dt=(Vsource(t)-Vprotected)/(R_total*C_effective)`; `Vprotected(t=0)=5.5 V`; load current is zero (a peak-charging sensitivity case, not a voltage-hold-up test); cutoff is perfectly isolating after a guessed delay.

The **historical workbook's calculated maximum rising trip** `5.9157205069 V` is only a *trigger-time reference in this synthetic waveform*. It is **NOT** a verified actual trip or a cap/limiter for the physical TS431 network. The `6.0 V` limit is TI TPS2116 absolute maximum stress, **NOT** a recommended continuous target. Normal-input no-nuisance up to 5.50 V remains required. The TPS2116 recommended operating limit is 5.5 V; the static slow-ramp design conflict established in T25CC still exists even if an individual modeled fault does not breach the 6 V absolute limit.

## Resistor and capacitor assumptions — do not accidentally convert to data-sheet guarantees

The real PCB `01_POWER.SchDoc` routes FSS2301S source `USB_OVP_IN` to drain `USB_VBUS_PROT`. Original FOSAN/LCSC exact FSS2301S product record [C2926138](https://item.szlcsc.com/3248238.html) reports RDS(on) **128 mΩ at specified VGS −2.5 V** (catalog condition, not a guaranteed worst-case R during a transient), Qg 4.5 nC typical and Ciss 327 pF typical. These latter values do **not** establish the 10k gate network's actual switching delay or its dynamic Miller plateau. R_total is swept 0.05/0.128/0.5/2 Ω as *illustrative lumped conduction/source resistance*, including a deliberately smaller 0.05Ω case. FSS2301S at hot/other VGS or source impedance might differ; there is no guaranteed minimum R specified by this simulation.

C_effective is swept 0.5/1/4.7/10/22/44 µF at the **protected VIN1 node** (not the `3V3_SYS` post-mux Cbus of T25CQ). It is NOT a catalog capacitor value, nor proof that `C_USB2` is populated. Actual TDK C_USB1/C_USB2 DC-biased min, manufacturer tolerances, thermal/age and assembly population remain unknown. A larger C may reduce this RC peak during a bounded transient but worsen inrush, diode stress, battery/USB transfer, footprint constraints and leakage. Under no circumstances infer design acceptance from the chosen illustrative C.

## Executed results — 504 distinct time-point cases, 72 root solves

Full 504-row `T25CS_SENSITIVITY_MATRIX.csv`, 72-case `T25CS_CUTOFF_BUDGET.csv`, source script, independent audit and SHA256 manifest are preserved. The Python stdlib computes the exact piecewise linear-source RC convolution, solves `Vprotected=6 V` with bisection, and independently cross-checks five trajectories with RK4 time integration (less than 20 µV discrepancy for all checked trajectories). The independent audit also checks monotonicity and cutoff flags across the 504 rows. Raw files are kept separate from conclusions.

**Illustrative 1 µs rise**, R_total=0.128Ω, C_eff=22µF, historical trip crossed at 0.063957µs after ramp start:

| Ideal complete cutoff delay after historical trip | Protected node peak, model only |
|---:|---:|
| 0.10 µs | 5.530431 V |
| 0.50 µs | 5.843740 V |
| 1.00 µs | **6.651681 V** — exceeds 6.0 V in this fixture |

The computed **maximum ideal cutoff delay after the historical crossing** to remain just under 6.0V, *with no safety margin*, is **0.620917 µs** for C=22µF. For the *same* 1µs rise and R=0.128Ω: C=1µF → 0.107410µs, C=10µF → 0.406996µs, C=44µF → 0.893245µs. A hypothetical R_total=0.05Ω/C=22µF allows only 0.374740µs. These should be used to design **the next model-validation measurement/simulation requirement**, not as a guaranteed allowable data-sheet cutoff spec.

## Necessary engineering interpretation

1. **Synthetic FAIL is not actual board FAIL**, just as synthetic under-limit is not board PASS. No official TS431BCX RFG + BC857BLT1G + FSS2301S vendor-model switching waveform is available/validated in this run. No physical `VGS`, `IK`, node coupling, gate charge, source impedance/ramp, Ceff or inrush are modeled; real MOSFET may turn off differently or pass energy after threshold. No LDO reverse-body, thermal, pass-through eFuse, mux current-conservation or MCU/SD tests are in the fixture.
2. **Architecture hazard remains:** T25CC's static conflict means a pass-through threshold calculated >5.50V cannot guarantee `VIN1≤5.50V` throughout *every* slow overvoltage while guaranteeing no nuisance cutoff at a 5.50V input. The 6V absolute rating must not be treated as acceptable normal regulation. A series Schottky SS14 is not an OVP voltage clamp.
3. **Vendor model gap confirmed as a specific blocker, not a reason to restart prior Windows scripts:** Official exact-part SPICE files for the three actual TS431/PNP/FET components have not been validated here. Onsemi's [BC857BLT1G product listing](https://www.onsemi.com/design/tools-software/product-recommendation-tools-plus/analog-bjt/products?filterAutoset=true&ftm=%28100.0%29&hfm=%28220.0%29&ict=%280.1%29&pd=%280.225%29&pol=%28PNP%29&simPartWpn=BC856BL&simParts=%28SBC856BLT3G%3BSBC856BLT1G%3BBC856BLT1G%3BBC856BLT3G%3BNSVBC856BM3T5G%3BBC856BM3T5G%3BBC857BLT3G%3BBC857BLT1G%3BSBC857BLT1G%29&vcm=%2845.0%29&veb=%285.0%29) advertises **SPICE Live Model** functionality but not a verified downloadable exact `BC857BLT1G.lib` with audited pin mapping. The older T25X investigation also failed to retrieve validated Taiwan Semi TS431BCX and FOSAN FSS2301S models. Do not silently substitute ST TS431 (1.24V) or an unrelated MOSFET model.
4. **Required next executable gate:** A vendor/model-proven, current-conserving OVP circuit fixture using pin-correct `TS431BCX RFG` (REF/K/A), `BC857BLT1G` (B/E/C), `FSS2301S` (G/S/D), Zener `BZT52C6V2-7-F` (SPICE A/K) and the literal R_K1..K3 / R_BASE / R_BE_PU / R_GATE_PD / R_HYST / R_TOP and R_BOTTOM networks. Define a physical *bounded* 12V source (slew/series R/max current/duration), effective C_USB1 and C_USB2 population, IC electrical/thermal envelope; record `USB_OVP_IN`, `USB_VBUS_PROT`, `OVP_K`, `OVP_REF`, `OVP_BASE`, `OVP_GATE`, `VGS`, `IK` and MOSFET source/drain current, including no-load and 280mA. A fallback protection architecture with true regulation must be considered if all required source stresses cannot be bounded safely. Separate test gate remains BAT loaded 1.80V→3V3 battery regulator and no-reset transfer with source current and Cbus_eff.
