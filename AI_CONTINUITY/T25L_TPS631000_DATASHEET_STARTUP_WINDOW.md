# T25L — TPS631000 data-sheet startup window and practical model scope

Date: 2026-09-24. Status: **data-sheet timing/feedback audit COMPLETE; board startup simulation or measurement NOT PASS**.

## Verified sources and exact project values

- TI [TPS631000 data sheet Rev. C (August 2026), pp. 1, 4–7](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps631000.pdf), current manufacturer's document. Table 5.5 typical conditions `VIN=3.8 V, VOUT=3.3 V, TJ=25 °C` unless noted. Specified **enable-to-start-of-ramp delay** `td(EN)=0.87 ms typical, 1.5 ms maximum` (no guaranteed minimum printed); **soft-start ramp** `td(ramp)=6.42 ms minimum, 7.55 ms typical, 8.68 ms maximum`. Its Figure 6-1 includes a current-clamp phase followed by reference ramp; the voltage trace is not necessarily a single linear slope, and startup time depends on load/capacitance.
- The device's recommended operating VIN is **1.6–5.5 V**, but TI's summary specifies **VIN > 1.65 V for start-up**, with UVLO rising threshold **1.5–1.599 V**, and EN must exceed **1.2 V** for guaranteed high (Table 5.5 / §6.3.2). These are distinct conditions. `01_POWER.SchDoc` ties `U_SYS EN` to `VIN_MUX`. Camelion two-AA nominal 3 V is above the threshold when fresh; **pulsed battery terminal voltage and mux voltage under load near end of discharge are unknown**, so actual reliable startup remains unverified.
- Actual feedback resistors from T25F are `R_FB_TOP=510 kΩ`, `R_FB_BOTTOM=91 kΩ`. With nominal values and TI `VFB=495/500/505 mV` min/typ/max, `Vout=VFB × (1+510/91)` = **3.269 / 3.302 / 3.335 V** *before* resistor tolerances, ripple and load regulation; these are static reference-derived numbers, not startup waveforms.
- TI recommends **effective** output capacitance at 3.3 V within `10.4–330 µF` and gives 16.9 µF nominal column. Project `C_OUT` is nominal **47 µF** (T25F) before DC bias and tolerance. Thus 47 µF on the BOM **does not prove** the effective minimum at 3.3 V.

## Timing interpretation and feasibility decision

- At the TI table's typical-condition timing, `0.87 + 7.55 = 8.42 ms`; combining listed maxima gives `1.5 + 8.68 = 10.18 ms`. These are a **data-sheet timing budget for the control sequence**, not guaranteed time for this board to attain final regulation under all source/load conditions. There is no printed minimum `td(EN)` to calculate a guaranteed earliest completed startup. A test of only 10 µs is ~842 times shorter than the combined typical sequence; 10 µs model smoke T25D reaching `VOUT=0.1182 V` is therefore **not an indication of faulty 3.3 V regulation**. TI's model/test startup waveform may differ from the data-sheet table and must be judged from completed traces.
- T25D's exact encrypted PSpice engine run used **30.98 s wall time for 10 µs** of its own isolated fixture. A roughly 10-ms controlled startup simulation would be **1000× longer in simulated time**; runtime scaling is not established and may not be linear. Blindly launching an unbounded full-startup transient is operationally unsound. Prefer a bounded, explicit wall-time experiment if needed; preserve completion markers, final voltage and time coverage before claiming PASS.
- For progress independent of the user's Windows PSpice, use a **separately labeled behavioral startup *timing envelope*** constrained by TI's stated delay/ramp and source/feedback thresholds. Do not invent internal control-loop dynamics or a board voltage trace from just those numbers. Keep real startup, transient dip and SD/OLED switched load response **OPEN** until exact-model Windows run across relevant milliseconds or bench capture is feasible; behavioral scenario output is an exploration rather than product qualification.
- The conditional OLED panel power accounting in T25K does not supply a carrier current-versus-time load for converter startup; the correct photographed four-pin module's input is unspecified. Continue to separate battery, USB, SD and OLED scenarios from validated hardware.
